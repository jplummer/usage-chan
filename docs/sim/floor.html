The flip & the floor

Two changes to the vertical dashboard, from 2026-11-09. The bars stop trading places when the 7-day window binds, and the bars run to the bottom row of the panel. Steady on the round floor was chosen and is now in screens.js and src/ui.cpp; this page is the record of the candidates, drawn by floor-variants.js. Each column is a candidate and each row is a state, so read across to compare candidates and down to see whether one candidate holds its meaning as the state changes.

1 · The flip

Shipped gives the wide column and the headline to whichever window the server names as binding. Rows one and four are the same layout with the two windows swapped, which is the screen that had to be reread.

The candidates keep 5-hour wide on the right and 7-day thin on the left, always, and are drawn on the chosen floor (section 2). The 7-day column always says how much is left. Extra usage (the outline, the mark, and the word) goes to whichever window binds.

Tinted keeps the headline with the 5-hour window. When the 7-day window binds, its title and amount take the color of its bar.

Steady is tinted, and the 5-hour headline is always the amount left. Shipped swaps it for a bare reset time below 40%, in an orange that can match the other bar. Here the reset time stays at the foot, and the headline takes its own bar's color when that is not green.

Cross looks like tinted until the 7-day window binds. Then the big headline leaves the 5-hour column and a smaller one appears beside the 7-day bar, in 9pt bold because the 12pt face does not fit that 84px slot. The 5-hour column drops to a small line. "out of credits" is 88px in that slot and loses its first 4px off the left edge.

2 · The floor

Shipped tracks stop at row 224 of 240. Chosen: tracks run to 240 with round bottoms, and the reset labels drop from y=216 to y=224. A mark that straddles the bar's end would be half off the panel, so it moves inside. Both columns use the tinted layout so only the floor differs. Not yet checked on the device: whether the bezel hides the last rows.
