Reading this page

the sim is the spec
These are the device's glyph bitmaps and LovyanGFX's own shape loops, pixel-doubled. A face that looks right here looks right on the panel, modulo backlight. The only things a hardware check adds are brightness and viewing angle.
label case
UPPER labels are the shipped convention; Title case is the candidate. Caps in a 16px face spend a lot of pixels shouting "5-HOUR" at a reader who already knows.
the em dash finding
The status line read "14m old — can't refresh", and Font2 has no em dash, so the device drew a space there. The sim showed it on its first render; the firmware now uses a comma. Font2 and the Free fonts are ASCII only, so punctuation stays inside ASCII.
