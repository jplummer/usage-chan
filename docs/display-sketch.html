The three treatments at the hard case

The awkward moment is when the tick sits within a pixel or two of the block edge — on pace exactly. Each treatment is drawn here at utilization 61% and elapsed 62%, one pixel apart on a 292-pixel bar.

What the geometry encodes

left edge of block = utilization
The remaining budget is anchored to the right, so spending pushes its left edge rightward. Both markers travel the same direction: time flies like an arrow.
tick position = elapsed ÷ window length
Deterministic, not estimated. It never jumps, never revises, and needs no burn-rate model — which is why it sidesteps the volatility that makes fuel-gauge projections untrustworthy.
tick left of edge = behind pace
You are spending slower than even. Calm, regardless of how little is left. This is what replaces a level-based colour threshold.
tick right of edge = ahead of pace
Burning faster than the window can carry. The one state worth a colour change, because it fires on the days it should and not on ordinary ones.
right edge = reset and exhaustion
If the tick reaches it before the block vanishes, you made it.
